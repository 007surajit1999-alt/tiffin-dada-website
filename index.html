Fan Favorites

Dishes people ask for again and again

Aloo Posto

Classic Bengali comfort food. Tender potatoes cooked in a rich, delicately sweet, and nutty poppy seed paste.

Kadahi Paneer

Soft paneer cubes tossed with crisp bell peppers and onions in a freshly ground, aromatic spiced tomato gravy.

Chana Bora Jhol

Traditional lentil dumplings simmered in a light, cumin-scented curry. A wholesome, home-style favorite.

Soya Chap Gravy

High-protein soya chaap cooked until tender in a robust, deeply spiced onion and tomato masala.

Chilli Mushroom

A fiery and flavorful Indo-Chinese hit. Plump mushrooms tossed with green chilies, garlic, and soy.

Begun Bharta

Smoky, fire-roasted eggplant mashed with mustard oil, pungent onions, and fresh green chilies.

Kacha Amaer Chatni

A sweet, tangy, and refreshing raw mango chutney tempered with Bengali five-spice (panch phoron).

Misti Doi

The quintessential Bengali dessert. Thick, creamy, caramelized sweet yogurt to perfectly round off your meal.

Macher Dim r Bora

Crispy, golden fritters made from fresh fish roe mixed with finely chopped onions, green chilies, and aromatic spices.

Macher Mathar Dal

A rich and comforting Bengali classic — roasted fish head slow-cooked with spiced moong dal, finished with ghee and warm spices.

Egg Kosha

Hard-boiled eggs pan-fried and enveloped in a thick, deeply caramelized, and spicy onion-tomato gravy.

Chicken Patla Jhol

A light, soupy, and incredibly comforting chicken curry cooked with large chunks of potatoes. True weekend nostalgia.

Chicken Dak Bunglow

A heritage recipe featuring slow-cooked chicken and potatoes in a rich gravy, finished with a boiled egg.

Doi Katla

Premium Katla fish steaks gently simmered in a smooth, mildly spiced, and luscious yogurt-based gravy.

Rui Macher Patla Jhol

A light, everyday Rohu fish stew cooked with seasonal vegetables and basic spices. Pure, healthy comfort.

Lal Misti / Gulab Jamun

Soft, melt-in-your-mouth deep-fried milk dumplings soaked in a warm, fragrant cardamom sugar syrup.
